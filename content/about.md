howdy, i'm gail!
about me
I’m a product designer who’s worn a lot of hats - software engineer, product manager, UX researcher - but the common thread? I love designing things people actually want to use.
Whether it’s hardware, software, or something in between, I blend design, engineering, and research to make tech feel less like tech and more like magic.

my design principles
1.  functionality
2. modularity
3. customizability
4. modesty
5. delight
